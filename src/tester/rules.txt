! Title: Rule-type tester
! Description: Rules that exist only so the tester page at /test can tell which
! rule types your blocker enforces. Everything here is scoped to /test/a/ or to
! the tester's own classes, so no real site is affected.
! Expires: 1 day
!
! Subscribe to this list in uBlock Origin (or add it as a blocklist here), then
! open /test and press Run. In uBO, paste it into "My filters" and tick "Trust
! my filters" — without that uBO throws away $replace, $urlskip and
! $uritransform, whichever list they came from.

! ---------------------------------------------------- 2.3 advanced matching
/test/a/*/method.json$method=post
/test/a/*/header.json$header=x-test-marker
/test/a/*/reqheader.json$requestheader=x-test-request
! $ipaddress is two-sided. Whichever of these two describes the address you
! opened the tester on should be blocked and the other should not, so a blocker
! that drops the option (neither blocked) and one that reads it as a plain block
! (both blocked) are both told apart from one that honours it.
/test/a/*/ipaddr-loopback.gif$image,ipaddress=loopback
/test/a/*/ipaddr-lan.gif$image,ipaddress=lan
! No $cname rule. It needs a real CNAME record pointing a first-party name at an
! ad host, which this server cannot create. The row says so rather than guessing.

! its own. On a shared page it hid nearly every other fixture and turned rows
! green that had done nothing.

{{TP}}#?#.t-if:if(.t-needle)
